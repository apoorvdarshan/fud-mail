# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming to participants of every background.
Critique code and ideas rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
sexualized conduct, spam, and publishing others' private information are not
acceptable in issues, pull requests, or other project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to apoorv@fud-ai.app. The sole maintainer,
Apoorv Darshan, reviews reports, protects reporter privacy where possible, and
may remove content, warn participants, or restrict participation based on the
severity and repetition of misconduct. Decisions may be appealed by replying
privately with additional context. These rules apply to the maintainer too.
